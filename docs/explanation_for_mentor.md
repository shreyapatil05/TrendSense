# Explanation for mentor

TrendSense forecasts whether a known Indian Gen Z fashion or beauty trend will strengthen, remain stable, or weaken over the next 14 or 28 days. It separately recommends INVEST, WAIT, or AVOID.

Scout gathers evidence. Five analysis modules calculate strength, lifecycle, authenticity, industry relevance, and commercial proxy. Decision combines fixed scores. Explainability shows why. Every source observation and forecast is stored. When the horizon ends, a future run becomes the outcome and is compared with the old prediction.

Accuracy means direction macro F1 and balanced accuracy, numeric MAE/RMSE, and top-trend Precision at K. Until real predictions mature, the system has evaluation capability but no proven accuracy.

Multiple agents are useful because collection, momentum, source agreement, business relevance, and decision policy have different inputs and failure rules. Data passes as a keyword-keyed record, with each stage adding fields.

The implemented system is hybrid ML and AI-assisted. A Random Forest learns
short-horizon direction from historical features. Hugging Face MiniLM checks
semantic relevance across text sources. The remaining agents orchestrate
collection, evidence, business safeguards, and explanation. It is automated
but not claimed to be autonomous Agentic AI.

WGSN conceptually blends broad quantitative and qualitative data with expert analysts. Trendalytics publicly emphasizes large digital datasets and predictive AI. Their internal algorithms are proprietary. TrendSense differs through narrow India scope, public inputs, exact formulas, explicit uncertainty, and reproducible evaluation.

Research contribution: an inspectable multi-source pipeline with immutable forecast history, predeclared ground truth, temporal validation, baselines, and failure analysis.
