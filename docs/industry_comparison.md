# Industry comparison

Public WGSN material says it combines proprietary and third-party data, expert analysis, macro research, consumer frameworks, and product feeds. Its disclosed feeds include social, sentiment, search, retail shelf data, and show imagery, followed by analyst review. Internal algorithms and full validation data remain proprietary. Source: https://www.wgsn.com/en/what-we-do/ai-data

Public Trendalytics material describes AI trend forecasting over millions of data points and highlights TikTok post/viewing velocity, consumer demand signals, and brand/retail use cases. Exact features, labels, algorithms, and independent validation are not public. Source: https://trendalytics.co/

| Dimension | TrendSense | WGSN | Trendalytics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Focus | 55 Indian Gen Z fashion/beauty terms | global multi-industry | fashion, beauty, home |
| Inputs | public search/content/editorial/pageview proxies | broad proprietary plus third-party and experts | large proprietary digital signal set |
| Historical memory | transparent JSONL | publicly states multi-year histories | not fully disclosed |
| Forecast method | disclosed rules, being validated | proprietary AI/data plus analyst-in-loop | proprietary AI algorithms |
| Explainability | exact weights and warnings | expert reports and guidance | platform insights |
| Reproducibility | intended academic strength | not externally reproducible | not externally reproducible |
| Scale | small prototype | commercial global scale | commercial scale |

TrendSense is not claimed to be better. Its contribution is transparency, India-specific scope, explicit uncertainty, reproducible temporal evaluation, and inspectable agent handoffs.
